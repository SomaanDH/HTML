Island

You decide to travel toward the mysterious island. After a long journey, you finally reach its sandy shore. The island looks completely untouched, with tall palm trees and strange plants everywhere. As you explore, you discover an old stone path leading into the jungle. Along the path, you find mysterious symbols carved into the rocks and hear strange sounds coming from deeper inside. At the end of the path, you see an ancient temple covered in vines. You wonder what secrets could be hidden inside and decide whether to enter the temple or return to the beach.
