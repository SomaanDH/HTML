Treasure

You follow the path toward the treasure and enter a small cave hidden inside the mountain. In the middle of the cave, you find an old wooden chest covered in dust. Your heart beats faster as you slowly open it. Inside, you discover gold coins, sparkling jewels, and an ancient map. It seems that someone hid the treasure here many years ago. You now have to decide whether to take the treasure and leave the cave or follow the ancient map to discover an even bigger secret.
