Village

You choose the path toward the village and walk down the mountain. After a while, you see small houses surrounded by farms and trees. The villagers are surprised to see you because they rarely see strangers coming from the mountain. An old villager welcomes you and tells you that there is a safe road leading back to the city. However, before you leave, the villager offers you a place to stay for the night or asks you to help with a mysterious problem in the village. You must decide what to do next.
