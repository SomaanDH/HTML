Cave

You decide to explore the mysterious cave hidden behind the trees. As you enter, the sunlight quickly disappears, and everything becomes dark and quiet. You carefully walk deeper into the cave and hear strange sounds coming from somewhere ahead. After a few minutes, you notice a small light shining from the end of the tunnel. You move closer and discover a room filled with old boxes and strange markings on the walls. One of the boxes appears to contain something valuable, but you are not sure if it is safe to open it. You must decide whether to follow the light deeper into the cave or turn back to the forest.
