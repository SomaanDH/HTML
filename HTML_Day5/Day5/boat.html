Boat

You choose to take the old boat and carefully push it into the water. At first, the boat moves slowly, but soon the waves carry you farther away from the beach. As you sail across the ocean, you notice dark clouds gathering in the sky. Suddenly, you see something strange floating in the water—a small wooden box. You pull it into the boat and discover an old map inside. The map shows a mysterious island nearby. You must decide whether to follow the map to the island or turn the boat back toward the beach.
